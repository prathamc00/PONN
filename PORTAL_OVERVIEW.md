# CRISMATECH E-Learning Portal

## What Is This Portal?

The CRISMATECH E-Learning Portal is an online learning platform run by **CRISMATECH Education**, a tech training company based in **KGF and Bengaluru**. It lets students learn technology skills through video-based courses, take quizzes, submit assignments, and earn certificates — all from a web browser, anytime and anywhere.

Think of it as a digital classroom: CRISMATECH's instructors create courses with video lessons and study materials, students enroll and learn at their own pace, and the platform tracks their progress and awards certificates when they pass assessments.

---

## Who Uses It?

The portal serves three types of users:

### Students
Students are the primary users. They register with their email (verified via OTP), enroll in courses, watch video lessons one by one, take quizzes, submit assignments, and download certificates when they complete courses successfully.

### Instructors
Instructors are the teachers who create and manage the courses. They upload video lessons, create quizzes, design assignments, and grade student submissions. Instructors must be approved by an admin before they can start teaching.

### Admins
Admins manage the entire platform. They approve instructor registrations, oversee all users and courses, monitor student activity, and view platform-wide analytics like enrollment numbers and completion rates.

## What Kind of Courses Are Offered?

The portal focuses on **technology and digital skills training**. Based on the platform's design and CRISMATECH's focus, the courses fall into areas like:

- **Programming and Software Development** — learning programming languages, writing code, building applications
- **Web Development** — front-end and back-end web technologies
- **Data Science and Analytics** — working with data, analysis, and visualization
- **Testing and QA** — software testing methodologies and tools
- **Cloud and DevOps** — cloud platforms, deployment, and infrastructure
- **Cybersecurity and Networking** — network fundamentals and security practices
- **Emerging Technologies** — AI, machine learning, and other modern tech topics

Each course is tagged with a **category** and a **difficulty level**:
- **Beginner** — for students just starting out, no prior experience needed
- **Intermediate** — for students with some background knowledge
- **Advanced** — for experienced learners looking to deepen their expertise

Courses are created by CRISMATECH's instructors and can include video lectures, downloadable PDF notes, quizzes, and hands-on assignments.

## What Is the Learning Experience Like?

### Step 1: Register and Verify Your Email
A new student signs up with their name, email, college, branch, and semester. The portal sends a 6-digit OTP code to their email to confirm they own it. Once verified, the account is created and they're logged in automatically.

### Step 2: Browse and Enroll in Courses
Students can browse all available courses on the Courses page. Each course shows its title, description, category, difficulty level, instructor name, and number of lessons. When a student finds a course they want, they click "Enroll" to join it.

### Step 3: Watch Video Lessons (One at a Time)
Inside a course, lessons are arranged in order. The first lesson is always unlocked, but each subsequent lesson only unlocks after the student finishes the previous one. This ensures students learn in the right sequence — they can't skip ahead.

Each lesson can include:
- A **video lecture** they can play, pause, rewind, and watch in full screen
- **PDF notes** they can download for offline study

As the student finishes each video, the platform marks that lesson complete and unlocks the next one. A progress bar shows how far they've come (e.g., "3 of 5 lessons complete — 60%"). When all lessons are done, a celebration confetti effect appears.

### Step 4: Take the Quiz
Once a student has watched **all** lessons in a course, the quiz becomes available. Before that, it stays locked with a clear message: "Complete all lessons first." The quiz can only be taken during a scheduled time window set by the instructor.

The quiz is a set of multiple-choice questions. The student answers all questions and submits. The platform checks answers and calculates a score and percentage.

To pass, a student needs at least **40%**.

### Step 5: Earn and Download a Certificate
If the student passes the quiz (40% or above), a certificate is automatically issued. The grade depends on the score:
- **A+** for 90% and above
- **A** for 80% and above
- **B+** for 70% and above
- **B** for 60% and above
- **C** for 50% and above
- **D** for 40% and above

The student can download their certificate as a high-quality image file (PNG) from the Certificate page. Each certificate has a unique ID code and can be verified by anyone — like an employer or university — through a public verification link, no login needed.

### Step 6: Submit Assignments and Get Feedback
Apart from quizzes, instructors can also give assignments. There are three types:
- **File Upload** — the student uploads a document (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, ZIP, or image file)
- **Case Study** — the student writes a detailed text answer
- **Code Submission** — the student pastes their code

After submitting, the instructor reviews the work, assigns marks, and writes feedback. The student can see their grade and the instructor's comments on their assignments page.

### Step 7: Track Attendance and Activity
The portal keeps a log of student activity — when they watch lessons, take quizzes, submit assignments, etc. Students can view this on the Attendance page to keep track of their participation.

## What Makes This Portal Special?

- **Learn at your own pace** — video lessons are available anytime; students progress through them sequentially but at whatever speed suits them
- **Real certificates with verification** — certificates aren't just PDFs; they have unique IDs that employers can verify online to confirm authenticity
- **Hands-on learning** — beyond just watching videos, students get assignments (including code submissions) that instructors personally grade with feedback
- **Structured learning path** — lessons unlock one by one so students build knowledge step by step instead of jumping around
- **Quiz completion gate** — students can't take the quiz until they've watched all lessons, ensuring they're actually prepared
- **Instructor-managed** — courses are created and maintained by real instructors who also grade assignments, not just automated content
- **Mobile-friendly** — the portal works on phones, tablets, and desktops with a modern, polished interface
